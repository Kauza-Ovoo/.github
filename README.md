# Kauza · Ovoo

**Digitalizing informal finance for West Africa.**

Ovoo brings two financial practices used every day across the UEMOA region, installment purchases (BNPL) and tontines (rotating savings groups), onto a single, secure digital platform.

</div>

<br />

## What we're building

In much of West Africa, people already buy on credit at trusted merchants and pool savings through informal tontines. These systems work because they're built on trust, but they're manual, hard to track, and impossible to scale safely. Ovoo digitizes both, giving buyers, merchants, and savings groups a platform that keeps the trust while adding transparency, security, and reach.

- 💳 **BNPL**: buyers pay in installments at partner merchants, with automated scheduling and payment tracking.
- 🔄 **Tontines**: rotating savings groups run digitally, contributions, payout order, and group trust made transparent.
- 🌍 **Built for the region**: FCFA-native, designed around real connectivity and payment constraints across UEMOA.
